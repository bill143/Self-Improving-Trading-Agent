# Closer Drill 2 — Multi-Location Close & The Clean No

**Agent:** `sales-closer-agent` · **Mode:** DRILL (constructed prospects — *not* real calls, no CRM records touched)
**Fixes two gaps found while prepping ops cycle 1:** the offer ladder priced only one location, and the decline path had never been rehearsed.

---

## A. DRILL — Multi-territory franchise owner

**Constructed prospect:** Marcus Reyes, owner-operator, Benjamin Franklin Plumbing — Orlando, Kissimmee and Sanford territories. 31 techs across 3 shops. ~$9.1M combined. Two dispatchers, one shared CSR pool. Reviews: Orlando 240/4.5, Kissimmee 61/4.3, Sanford 44/4.7 — newest review 5 weeks old. Uses ServiceTitan, one tenant, three business units.
*Why this prospect:* 3 of our 32 leads are held cluster siblings. The likeliest first close in this pipeline is a multi-territory owner, and "what about my other two shops?" is a close-killer if I haven't priced it.

*(A = closer, M = Marcus. Steps 1–4 run as written in the playbook; picking up mid-discovery where this scenario diverges.)*

**A:** Before we go further — how many locations do you run, and which brands?
**M:** Three. All Ben Franklin. Orlando, Kissimmee, Sanford.
**A:** Same ServiceTitan tenant or three?
**M:** One tenant, three business units.
**A:** That's the answer I wanted. Do the three share a customer database, or does Sanford only see Sanford?
**M:** Shared tenant, but nobody's touched Sanford's old records since we bought it in '22.
**A:** So how many total records, and how many are dormant — inquired six-plus months ago, never invoiced?
**M:** Twenty-something thousand total. Dormant I couldn't tell you.
**A:** Let's not guess. Can you pull "no invoice, first contact before December," all three units, while we talk? Ninety seconds.
**M:** *(pause)* …Sixty-one hundred. Huh.
**A:** That's your own number, not mine — and it just became the whole conversation. Which of the three has the most capacity to absorb work tomorrow?
**M:** Sanford. Orlando's slammed, Kissimmee's fine.

*(Discovery completes the 17-field scorecard per unit where it differs: spend, leads, booking %, ticket, close %, capacity per shop.)*

### The pitch adjustment
> "You know how you told me Sanford's been coasting on whatever walks in since you bought it? Sixty-one hundred dormant records across the tenant — and the shop with the most room on the board is the one with the least attention. So we don't launch three at once. We launch Sanford first, throttled to what its board can take, in your Sanford manager's name. You see it work in your own dispatch board before you've spent a dollar on the other two."

### The pricing question, and the answer
**M:** So what's this, fifteen hundred times three?
**A:** No. Here's how I price it and why. The build is per location — separate database scrub, separate service-manager name on the messages, separate review flow per Google profile, because Sanford's 44 reviews and Orlando's 240 are different problems. But the setup, the sequence logic and the reporting are built once and reused. So it's **$1,500 for the first location, $1,000 a month for each additional location on the same tenant.** Three shops is $3,500 a month, not $4,500.
> **And you don't have to buy three today.** Sanford alone is $1,500. If thirty days on Sanford does what it's built to do, you add the other two at a thousand each and I'll have already proven it on your smallest shop instead of your biggest. If it doesn't, you're out one month on one location.

**M:** Why would you leave money on the table like that?
**A:** Because a three-shop launch with one dispatcher pool is how agencies drown a client in month one and lose all three. I'd rather have Sanford working than Orlando angry.

*(Temperature check → 8. "Why aren't you a 1?" → he sells himself on the dormant number he pulled. "What would make you a 10?" → "seeing it on one shop first." That objection is already the offer. Re-ask → 10. Close per §7: three-step frame → terms aloud → "the investment is $1,500 a month for Sanford" → silence.)*

## B. DRILL — The clean no (two re-loops, then stop)

**Constructed prospect:** Dale Kupfer, owner, One Hour Heating & Air of Englewood OH. 9 techs. Wife Trina does the books. Refinanced two trucks in February.

**A:** On a scale of 1 to 10, where do you fall?
**D:** Six.
**A:** Why aren't you a 1?
**D:** Because the database thing is real. I know it's sitting there. And I liked that you didn't promise me numbers.
**A:** So what would make you a 10?
**D:** Money. February gutted us, I refinanced two trucks. Fifteen hundred a month right now is a truck payment.
**A:** Other than the cash, is there anything else between you and starting today?
**D:** No, that's it.
**A:** *(re-loop 1 — scope, never term, never a free month)* Then let's take scope off it, because I'd rather start smaller than not start. Reactivation only, no review system — $1,000 a month, and it's the half that works a list you already paid for instead of new spend. Where's the number?
**D:** …Still a six and a half. Trina would kill me.
**A:** *(re-loop 2 — isolate whether it's cash or the decision-maker)* Fair. Is it that the thousand isn't there, or is it that you and Trina haven't had the conversation?
**D:** Both, honestly. It's not there till the cooling season kicks.
**A:** **Then I'm not going to sell you.** You've got a cash problem, not a marketing problem, and me putting a thousand a month on top of two truck payments in April doesn't fix either one. I'd rather you tell me no than yes-and-cancel in thirty days.
> "Here's what I'd like to do instead. First hot week is usually mid-June for you. Can I call you the second week of June, when the phones are ringing and the cash looks different? And in the meantime — free, no strings — pull that dormant list yourself and have Sherry text the last hundred people who never got invoiced. It's the same idea done by hand. If it works, you'll know what I'm selling is real."

**D:** …Yeah. Call me in June.

**Recorded as:** `update_lead` → **DECLINED**, note: *"Cash constraint — refinanced 2 trucks Feb, no budget until cooling season. Decision shared with wife Trina (bookkeeper). Not a no on the mechanism: temp 6.5 after scope-down to $1,000 reactivation-only. Callback second week of June, lead with 'did you text the dormant hundred?' NOT DNC — no opt-out requested."*

### Drill self-audit — §B
| Step | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 6 TEMP CHECK | 9 | Two re-loops, then hard stop. Concession was **scope ($1,500→$1,000), never term, never the 30-day out, never a free month** — playbook §7.5 held. |
| 7 CLOSE | n/a | Correctly not attempted. **No payment processed = correct outcome, not a failed one.** |
| **New rules proved** | — | 1) Walking away from an owner who can't afford it protects the Phase-2 referral network — this niche talks to itself. 2) A DECLINED note must carry four things: **verbatim reason, temperature score at stop, decision-maker, callback date + the exact re-open line.** 3) Giving away the manual version of Pillar 1 on the way out costs nothing and makes the June call warm. |

## C. ADDENDUM to `phase7-sales-playbook` §7.5 — offer ladder (multi-location)

| Situation | Investment | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Single location, Pillars 1+2 | **$1,500/mo** | Unchanged |
| Single location, scope-down floor | **$1,000/mo** (reactivation only) | The *only* permitted concession. Never term, never the 30-day out, never a free month |
| Each additional location, same software tenant | **+$1,000/mo** | Build is per location; setup + logic + reporting reused once |
| 3+ locations | **Land-and-expand: start on ONE**, the shop with the most board capacity | Never launch 3 at once against a shared dispatcher pool |
| Multi-location expansion trigger | 30-day review with real numbers | Same gate as the Pillar 3 upsell |

**Qualifying question promoted into the discovery scorecard (now field 18):** *"How many locations do you run, which brands, and is it one software tenant or several?"* — asked **before** any number is quoted. This also doubles as the release condition for the held cluster siblings (Orlando ×2, Augusta ×1) that `appointment-setter-agent` is holding in NEW.

**Standing guardrails unchanged:** no invented case studies (we have zero clients and say so), no revenue guarantees, terms stated aloud before any card is touched, "am I talking to an AI?" → "yes," immediately and plainly.
